FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers to Important Questions.

What is CFO AI? ↓

CFO AI is an intelligent financial operating system for financial management, accounting preparation, document intelligence, and regulatory awareness designed specifically for businesses operating in Indonesia.

Is CFO AI an accounting firm? ↓

No. CFO AI provides software and AI-assisted workflows. Professional accounting, tax, and legal services are separate.

Can CFO AI replace my accountant? ↓

No. CFO AI eliminates the tedious administrative burden of collecting receipts, matching bank transactions, and building reports so your accountant can focus on tax optimization, strategic review, and statutory filings.

Can employees submit documents through Telegram? ↓

Yes. Authorized team members can submit vendor invoices and expense receipts through an authorized Telegram workflow directly into your company review queue.

Can I use ChatGPT or Claude with CFO AI? ↓

Claude is supported today via our official Model Context Protocol (MCP) OAuth connector. An authorized ChatGPT Action integration is currently in architecture for a future update.

Will CFO AI support WhatsApp? ↓

WhatsApp document collection is currently on our product development roadmap and planned for an upcoming release.

Can CFO AI notify me about KBLI or tax changes? ↓

Yes. CFO AI monitors Indonesian regulatory updates (including PMK 81/2024 tax schedules) and tracks compliance deadlines on your dedicated company calendar.

Can I upload bank statements? ↓

Yes. Direct statement parsing is available for BCA corporate CSV exports and Permata PDF statements, with atomic confirmation preventing duplicate transactions. Additional Indonesian banks are being added.

Is my company's data separated from other companies? ↓

Yes. CFO AI enforces strict multi-tenant boundary isolation across workspaces. Personal accounts and company workspaces are completely segregated.

How long is the free trial? ↓

You receive seven full days (168 hours) of complete trial access upon account activation, with full access to plan features within applicable trial limits.

Do I need a credit card to start? ↓

No credit card is required to create an account and activate your 7-day free trial.

Does CFO AI automatically file taxes? ↓

No. CFO AI compiles and reconciles all transaction records, linked receipts, and withholding estimates into a standardized package for your licensed tax consultant to file.
