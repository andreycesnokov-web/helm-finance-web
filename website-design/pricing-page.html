Common Questions About Pricing & Trials

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Which plan is right for my company?

If you have a compact PT or PT PMA with under 1,000 monthly transactions and simply need structured financial visibility, Starter ($59) is ideal. If you want automatic BCA and Permata bank statement reconciliation, employee receipt intake via Telegram, and Indonesian tax calendar tracking, Control ($149) is our most popular tier. For growing enterprises with higher transaction volume and complex audit requirements, select Pro ($299).

What happens when my 7-day free trial ends?

Because we do not require a credit card at signup, your card will never be charged automatically. At the end of the 168-hour trial, you can choose to activate your monthly subscription to keep access, or export your reconciled records with zero penalty.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can switch between Starter, Control, and Pro directly from your company settings at any time. Prorated adjustments are calculated automatically.

Does my external accountant need their own paid license?

No. CFO AI is designed to assemble monthly 1-click export packages (CSV, trial balances, and attached receipt PDFs) that you can hand off to your external accountant without paying for an additional seat.
