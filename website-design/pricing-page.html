QUESTIONS ABOUT BILLING & TRIALS

Common Questions About Pricing & Limits

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Which plan is right for my company?

If you operate a single PT or PT PMA with up to 5 team members and need cash visibility, Starter ($59) is ideal. If you require multi-step spend approvals, automated BCA and Permata bank statement reconciliation, employee receipt workflows via Telegram, and full Indonesian tax calendar tracking, Control ($149) is our flagship and recommended tier. For growing enterprises managing multiple companies (up to 5 entities) with high transaction volumes and external API integrations, select Pro ($249).

What happens when my 7-day free trial ends?

Because we do not require a credit card at signup, your card will never be billed automatically. At the end of the 168-hour trial window, you can choose to activate your monthly subscription to continue logging new entries, or export your reconciled data with zero penalty.

What happens if my company reaches its monthly usage limits?

If your transaction volume or document upload count approaches your plan limit, company administrators receive a proactive notification. Your existing financial records, dashboards, and export tools remain fully accessible. You can upgrade to a higher plan anytime to immediately restore capacity.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes. You can switch between Starter, Control, and Pro directly from your company billing settings at any time. When upgrading, new capacity takes effect immediately. When canceling, your access continues through the end of the paid billing period.

Does my external accountant require a separate paid user seat?

No. You can either invite your accountant to your workspace within your included seat allowance (5, 10, or 30 seats), or generate 1-click monthly export packages (CSV, trial balance, and organized receipt attachments) and email them without extra cost.
