QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about CFO AI, trials, limits, and workflows.

What exactly is CFO AI?

CFO AI is an intelligent financial operating system for business owners operating in Indonesia. It connects cash visibility, multi-channel invoice intake, bank reconciliation (BCA, Permata), accounting preparation, and Indonesian tax monitoring into one unified workspace.

Why do I need CFO AI if I already employ an accountant?

Your external accountant handles compliance filings, year-end financial statements, and statutory audits. CFO AI helps owners and operational managers control day-to-day cash flow, approve team expenses, track runway, and prepare organized monthly handoff packages with zero paperwork scramble.

Do I need a credit card to start the 7-day free trial?

No. You get 7 full days (168 hours) of complete trial access on Starter, Control, or Pro with zero payment card required at signup. You can invite your team, upload invoices, and test reconciliation immediately.

Which Indonesian banks and statement formats are supported?

CFO AI supports direct statement imports for Bank Central Asia (BCA KlikBisnis / Corporate CSV and PDF), Bank Permata, and standard CSV format exports from Bank Mandiri and other commercial banks.

How does the Telegram receipt workflow work?

Authorized team members send photos of receipts or vendor invoices to your company's dedicated Telegram bot. The document lands directly in your review queue with pre-extracted amounts, DPP, and PPN 11%, without giving staff access to bank balances.

What happens when I reach my monthly usage limits?

When approaching plan limits (such as monthly transactions or document uploads), the platform notifies administrators. Critical financial records and existing data remain accessible; you can upgrade to a higher tier anytime to continue unmetered intake.

Does CFO AI automatically file tax returns with the DGT / Pajak?

No. CFO AI prepares your records, verifies 16-digit NPWPs, calculates withholding schedules, and tracks CoreTax PMK 81/2024 deadlines. Official statutory filings are submitted through your certified tax consultant (*Konsultan Pajak*) or official government portals.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly within your company billing settings. When canceling, you retain access until the end of the paid billing period.
