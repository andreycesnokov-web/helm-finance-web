How profit is counted

  • Revenue counts in the month you invoice or deliver — not when the money arrives.
  • Costs count in the month you get the goods or service, including bills not yet paid.
  • Equipment over Rp 5M becomes an asset and is spread over its life as depreciation.
  • Loans, investor money and moves between your own accounts are never revenue or cost.
  • Taxes you withhold for others (PPh 21, PPh 23) are not your cost.

Set up for your type of business

Every category belongs to one group: revenue, direct cost, operating cost, asset purchase, funding, tax or transfer. CFO AI picks the groups from your KBLI codes — here, services and vending.

A restaurant would see food and drink cost as a direct cost. A rental business, property upkeep and agent fees. A developer, land and construction, with revenue counted when units are handed over.
