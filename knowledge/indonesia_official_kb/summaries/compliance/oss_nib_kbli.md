# OSS, NIB, KBLI and licensing - summary

> Orientation note written from official sources on 2026-09-02. **Not a citation and not legal advice.** Every figure is a candidate awaiting licensed review. Go to the primary regulation before relying on anything here.

## What we established

Risk-based business licensing via OSS implements **UU 11/2020** (Cipta Kerja). Business activities are
classified into four risk tiers - Rendah (R), Menengah Rendah (MR), Menengah Tinggi (MT), Tinggi (T)
- and low / low-medium risk licences can be issued directly through OSS. The **NIB** functions as the
single business identity. Activity classification uses **KBLI 2020**, browsable on the OSS portal
(`OSS_KBLI_001`).

OSS maintains its own regulation index (`OSS_003`), which is the best entry point for primary text.
