# LKPM and BPJS - summary

> Orientation note written from official sources on 2026-09-02. **Not a citation and not legal advice.** Every figure is a candidate awaiting licensed review. Go to the primary regulation before relying on anything here.

## What we established

**LKPM.** Investment companies must report investment activity periodically. The cited basis is
**UU 25/2007 Pasal 15**, with procedure in **Peraturan BKPM 6/2020**. A 2021 announcement
(`OSS_LKPM_003`) describes an obligation for businesses with investment value above Rp500,000,000 to
submit quarterly, with a deadline of the 10th of the month after the period, and describes escalating
warnings leading to possible licence revocation for non-compliance. Submission is online.

**BPJS.** **UU 24/2011** obliges employers to register themselves and their workers. BPJS
Ketenagakerjaan registration uses Form 1 (employer) plus Form 1a (worker) and requires a copy of the
**NIB or business licence and the NPWP** (`BPJSTK_002`, `BPJSTK_004`). Membership is described as
effective once the form is received and the first contribution is paid.

## What is unresolved

- **The LKPM threshold and deadline come from a period-specific 2021 announcement.** They must not be
  generalised to today. The current Perka has not been checked.
- **BPJS Kesehatan was not collected at all** - only Ketenagakerjaan.
- PER BPJS 01/2016 may be superseded; not verified.
- Contribution rates not collected.

## Why this matters to the product

BPJS registration requiring NIB + NPWP is a concrete link between the Company Vault and compliance:
the documents the vault holds are the inputs to an obligation the business actually has.
