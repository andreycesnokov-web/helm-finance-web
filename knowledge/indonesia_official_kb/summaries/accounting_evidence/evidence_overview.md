## The distinction that matters

Several of these are **accounting controls**, not legal obligations. "A payable needs a payment
proof" is a sound internal control and it is what our product already enforces - but we have no
Indonesian source saying it is legally required, and the knowledge base must not imply otherwise.

The reviewer question is precisely this: which of these are legal retention obligations, and which
are good practice we are choosing to enforce?
